# Sump pit gravel procurement

Find the lowest total cost for suitable gravel for the fixed rectangular **32 × 24-inch sump pit**. Start with a local bulk-pickup quote from Tri City Rock, then compare it with self-filled bags from Lyngsø. Bulk pickup is a promising low-cost option, but the cheapest supplier has not been confirmed by current quotes.

This document covers purchasing and transport. See [sumppit.md](sumppit.md) for the dimensions, gravel calculation, and installation considerations.

## Quantity to quote

The pit depth is the drum height plus 12 inches: **43.50 inches** using the published 31.50-inch drum height.

| Item | Provisional quantity |

| Convenient bulk quote quantity | Approximately 0.6 cubic yard |

**These are quote quantities, not a finalized order.** They assume a tapered drum with a 21.26-inch top outside diameter and an illustrative 18-inch bottom outside diameter, with no soil cap. VEVOR's linked image gives the overall width and height but does not label the bottom diameter; overall width may include the lid. Confirm the drum's actual top and bottom outside diameters before finalizing the order. A permitted soil cap reduces the gravel requirement.

## Material to request

Ask for **clean, washed, angular drain rock within the ⅛–¾-inch size range**, consistent with the specification recorded in the installation document. Have the supplier confirm the grading and washed condition; a product name alone is insufficient.

Avoid Class II base rock, material containing dirt or fines, rubble, and oversized rocks. The installation document also records pea gravel as permitted by the manual, although angular crushed stone is its preferred option.

## Keep transport costs low

About 15–16 cubic feet of rock can weigh well over **1,000 lb**, depending on the material. Get the supplier's estimated load weight and check the vehicle's remaining payload after passengers, tools, and containers. Divide the load into smaller trips if needed.

A borrowed suitable pickup can make bulk collection economical. If hiring a vehicle or making several trips is necessary, compare that total with delivery. Confirm that the supplier can load the vehicle or containers in the proposed way before arriving.

## Before ordering

1. Confirm the drum measurements and update the gravel quantity in the installation document.
2. Get Tri City Rock's pickup and delivery quotes for the resulting quantity.
3. Confirm Lyngsø's self-filled-bag price and compare the total including travel and any bag charges.
4. Choose the lowest total cost that supplies the specified material and fits the transport plan.
